Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about my services.

What is your development process?

My development process is structured into phases: Discovery (understanding your requirements), Planning (architecture and tech stack), Development (agile sprints with regular updates), Testing (comprehensive QA), and Deployment (release to Play Store with monitoring).

Which technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in Kotlin and Android development, particularly with Jetpack Compose, MVVM architecture, Firebase, and Room database. I'm also experienced with Clean Architecture, CI/CD pipelines, and modern security practices.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity: Simple apps take 2-4 weeks, medium complexity apps take 1-2 months, and enterprise solutions take 2-4 months. I always provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes! All my packages include post-launch support. I provide bug fixes, app updates, performance monitoring, and feature enhancements. Extended support can be agreed upon based on your needs.

Can you work with an existing codebase?

Absolutely! I have extensive experience refactoring and optimizing existing applications. I can work with your current team to improve code quality, add features, optimize performance, and modernize legacy code.

Do you provide design services as well?

While my primary focus is development, I can create UI/UX designs using modern design tools like Figma. For projects requiring extensive design work, I collaborate with professional UI/UX designers.
